Personal Google Workspace Assistant

This is a personal-use application that connects to its owner's own Google Account to help manage their Gmail, Calendar, and Tasks through a private automation assistant.

It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. It runs only on infrastructure controlled by its owner, and accesses only the owner's own accounts.

What it does

  • Reads and summarizes the owner's own email
  • Forwards specific order notifications to the owner's own chat
  • Reads the owner's own calendar events and task lists
