Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

This Google OAuth application is a personal-use integration. It is not a commercial product, is not distributed to the public, and is not used to collect, process, or share data belonging to anyone other than its owner.

What this app does

It connects, via Google's OAuth protocol, to the owner's own Google Account(s) to read and manage their own Gmail, Calendar, and Tasks data as part of a personal automation assistant. All processing happens on infrastructure controlled by the owner.

Data use

Data retrieved through this app (such as email content or calendar events) is used only to perform actions the owner has requested of their own assistant (for example, summarizing messages or forwarding specific order notifications to the owner's own chat). It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or made available to any third party.

Data retention

Only the minimum data needed to perform a requested task is retained, and only on the owner's own infrastructure. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are revocable at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Limited Use disclosure

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the support address listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
